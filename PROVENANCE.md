# Provenance and AI assistance

The revised release consolidates mathematical work from the project's 14 October 2026 drafts, *Explicit Nim-Sequences and Transients for the Subtraction Games {a,4a,c}* and *Non-Pure Periodicity and Affine Word Classifications for Subtraction Games with Moves a,4a,c*, and the complete manuscript submitted for this revision.

The reused material includes the outer residue constructions, the a=2 formulas, the 89 affine records and 89 seam words, and the universal pure-period obstruction. The completed project also supplies the upper/lower half-strip, balanced-necklace and integer-seam constructions. This revision preserves those definitions and theorem formulas, explains their dependency chain, and improves verification of the association between seam labels and parameter pairs.

The mathematical arguments, verification code and manuscript were generated using ChatGPT and Codex. The human project organizer initiated and steered tasks, requested explanations and revisions, and arranged publication; no human contribution to the mathematical proofs is claimed. This note does not invent independent human derivations, external peer review, or Lean verification. The supplied Python programs are exact executable checks whose unbounded scope relies on the readable reduction proofs in the paper.

The previous manuscript and release files were preserved in a separate local backup before revision. The public-facing supplement deliberately omits development logs and obsolete working notes: every required mathematical argument is printed in the revised paper or represented by its explicitly identified defining data and checkers. The historical drafts are acknowledged here, not cited as unavailable proof premises.
